Insane Productivity: Module 2 Notes

Mon 09 February 2026 in Insane Productivity
#productivity

This module is where we identify what’s overpowering and suppressing your great super powers.

Part of your job is identifying what your super powers are. Then you are tasked to strengthen them and protect them from what can hinder and sabotage them. That is what we will learn in this module — what your kryptonite is and how to rid and block yourself from it once and for all.

Distractions are your kryptonite. 89% of workers don’t complete top three tasks each day due to distractions.

It’s not overabundance of technology that’s the problem, it’s the overconsumption of it.

Communications and distractions are becoming even more dense and intense.

Go to war against distractions. Focus is primarily about eliminating distractions.

Some tips:

  • Don’t take your phone into meetings
  • Put your phone out of reach
  • Designate no screen zones, time periods and/or days
  • Remove Facebook and other social networks from your mobile phone altogether

We are given what's called a "Digital Addiction Worksheet" to fill out. Here's a list of the items I put a checkmark on:

The plan given is to work on three of these each week until we take care of all of them. I'm starting with the top 3 and going in order to the bottom. Will track with 🅿️ emoji for in progress and ✅ emoji for completed.

  • Do you sometimes bring your phone, tablet, or laptop with you while you sit on the toilet? 🅿️ (Week of 2/15/26)
  • If you have to wait in line for more than 2 minutes do you often pull out your smartphone? 🅿️
  • If are waiting at a stoplight that looks like it will be red for more than 30 seconds, do you often pull out your smartphone? 🅿️
  • Do you check your phone while in church?
  • Does the topic around what you have posted or seen on social media come up constantly in your real-life conversations?
  • Are there more than two portable electronic devices within reach of your bed?
  • When you post an update/video on social media are you disappointed if no one likes or comments on it? (Be honest!)
  • Do you often check your social accounts when you are our with others socially?
  • Have you ever described what you are eating or posted a picture of your meal on social media?
  • Do you ever tell a friend in person if they saw your post on social media to learn what’s new with you?
  • Do you check email, text, or social media accounts before you brush your teeth in the morning?
  • Do you spend more time on your social media pages than you do in the presence of actual people?
  • Have you ever asked, “Are we friends (on FaceBook/Instagram/TikTok/Twitter, etc)?”
  • Do you check your email, text, and/or social media accounts one last time after you brush your teeth at night?
  • Do you text, check email, and/or scroll on social media while driving even though you know it’s worse than if you were driving drunk?
  • Do you have more than five tabs open in your browser at a time?
  • Can you type text messages faster than you can handwrite the same words?
  • Do you keep scrolling, checking/ answering emails/DM’s/texts while you’re sitting in your car in your driveway or garage when you get home?
  • Do you deny you have an addiction to your smartphone? (First stage of any type of addiction)
