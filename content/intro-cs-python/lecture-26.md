### Simulations

Topic useful for many domains

* __Computationally__ describe the world using __randomness__
* One very important topic relative to many fields of study
    * Risk modeling and analysis
    * Reduce complex models

Idea:

* __Observe an event__ and want to calculate something about it
* Using computation, __design an experiment__ of that event
* __Repeat the experiment__ k many times (make a simulation)
* __Keep track__ of the outcome of your event
* After k repetitions, __report the value of interest__
