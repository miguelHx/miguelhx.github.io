Insane Productivity: An Introduction

Mon 02 February 2026 in Insane Productivity
#productivity

Taking Darren Hardy's Insane Productivity course. I paid around $3,000 for this course. I know, that's a lot. It's advertised as the best knowledge about productivity out there. I'm about to see if it's worth it. I plan on writing a review at the end.

I will be taking notes on each video lesson and posting it here on my blog. I want to share what I learn and leave a digital trail. I can review them later as-needed.

Here are my notes on the introductory video:

Welcome to Insane Productivity

What are the commonalities of the most successful people in the world?

  1. They are rapid learners. They read they study and they invest in advisors. They reach out for counsel. They’re always seeking that success edge. That little bit more knowledge. That little insight, that tip, that distinction that will give them the advantage. When they see an opportunity to learn they are the first in line every time.
  2. They decide. High achievers have the confidence and courage to step forward.
  3. They act. Ultimately it comes down to action. Action is the last and most important attribute that successful people possess that others don’t.

We have 3 options to extract the info from this course:

  1. Learn
  2. Study
  3. Master

We’re free to choose. High achievers would want to master important information.
