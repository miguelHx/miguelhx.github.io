This module is where we identify what’s overpowering and suppressing your great super powers.

Part of your job is identifying what your super powers are.  Then you are tasked to strengthen them and protect them from what can hinder and sabotage them.  That is what we will learn in this module — what your kryptonite is and how to rid and block yourself from it once and for all.

Distractions are your kryptonite.  89% of workers don’t complete top three tasks each day due to distractions.

It’s not overabundance of technology that’s the problem, it’s the overconsumption of it.

Communications and distractions are becoming even more dense and intense.

Go to war against distractions.  Focus is primarily about eliminating distractions.

Some tips:

* Don’t take your phone into meetings
* Put your phone out of reach
* Designate no screen zones, time periods and/or days
* Remove Facebook and other social networks from your mobile phone altogether
