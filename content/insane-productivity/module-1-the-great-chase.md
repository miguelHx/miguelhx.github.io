Title: Insane Productivity: Module 1 - The Great Chase
Date: 2026-02-07 14:00
Category: Insane Productivity
Tags: productivity

Achievers are wired to achieve, not to retire/relax all the time.

We NEED the chase!  The chase is what gives us life, gives us juice.  The chase is our creative spirit getting a chance to be expressed, stretched and realized.  The chase is the means AND the end.  You can give it different semantics like pursuit or purpose or vision or mission.  Even proverbs from the Bible says that without vision people perish.

Don’t want what you don’t want.  The CHASE ends when you are dead.

Advice: If you don’t want to chase your current goals, then find something else to chase.

Pick your chase wisely — you will get ALL IN.

Be sure your motivations are intrinsic vs. extrinsic.

You’ll spend 80-90% of your life working/CHASING…. Make sure it fits the life you want.  Software engineering is the life I want.  I choose to go all in.

Identify YOUR unique strengths.

Stay in your Circle of Competency (Strength).

Don’t get into a business you have no business being in (according to your unique strengths).

My strengths are my software development aptitude, my writing ability, my learning ability, my time management, my health habits, and my career experience in software engineering.

Backfill your weaknesses (Hiring other people to fill in your weaknesses is one way).

Identify your unique strength — then strengthen it further.  My unique strength is my software technical skills.  I’m working on strengthening it further by doing the OSSU curriculum.

Summary:

1. You are going to be chasing
2. Choose your chase carefully
3. Stay in your circle of competency/strength area

Exercise:  Write a description of your best self. When are you at your best?  What does that look like?  Describe it.  Then also answer the question:  What is my best life?

My best self is when I’m in a flow state of mind, working on a programming problem.  I am dialed in, on my laptop, dedicating most of my brain resources to solving a technical problem.  I have a notebook at my disposal for jotting anything down.  I also have the internet at my fingertips.

I am also my best self when I am learning something new in my areas of interest, which are software engineering, computer science, programming, writing, etc.

My best life is being a highly respected software engineer, who works on solving technical problems along with people problems.
