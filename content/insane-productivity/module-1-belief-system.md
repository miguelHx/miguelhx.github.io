Our beliefs, however they got installed, however they were planted, shape our entire destiny from that point forward.

Everything we hear, feel, see, etc. ends up becoming a filter through our beliefs.  So it’s important to learn how to change your beliefs to change your life.

If you learn how to change the beliefs of those that will join your business, and understand what might be holding them back, what unconscious belief prison bars are holding them and their potential back, you can change their lives.

Acceptance is not a choice, but a requirement for survival. You name, language, where you live, school, etc. were decided for you.

Ever wonder why you have irrational doubts, fears, inhibitions to harmless activities?
It’s called the __cave syndrome__.  Our ancestors walked by a cave, a tiger came out of it, and they got mauled.  So ever since, our ancestors have been scared of caves, and even up to now, when tigers don’t live in caves anymore.

Because that story has been passed down, you still feel that fear, even though that’s not the reality anymore.

If you’ve ever had an activity that made you feel small, or rejected, or you felt some emotional pain, that becomes a cave.  And now you start judging and comparing every other experience with that.  So you end up collecting all these caves in your mind.  This could be something as simple as picking up the phone or introducing yourself to a stranger.

Here’s a really important factor:  Intelligence does not matter.  You might come across someone who is a PhD or intellectual who has some crazy beliefs.
Your beliefs become your identity.
Once beliefs are installed, you can only see the world through this prism.

You cannot see what it is you’re not looking for.  And you’re looking for what you only believe to see.
It’s called the __Reticular Activating System__ (RAS).  It’s a part of the brain that’s responsible for filtering out information that does not match what you believe to be true.
Your prism becomes your prison, and this is what’s holding you back.

Limiting beliefs are continually and perpetually reinforced.  We interpret information that comes into our senses and mold it to fit the way we want the world to be and not necessarily how it really is.  Process called __Belief Confirmation__.

How this works:

* __Seeking__ (RAS):  Looking for that which reinforces what you already believe; self talk
    * If you believe the world is dangerous, that the economy is bad, you will continually see all the things out there that reinforce that belief.
* __Filtering__ (Confirmation Bias): Ignore data that contradicts
    * This is why pundit debates can be useless. One side is not even listening to the other side’s arguments
* __Recalling__ (Hindsight bias): Selected memories function as "proof"
* __Grooving__ (Psychological ruts):  Return to core beliefs in stress/uncertainty
* __Identity__:  How you see yourself
    * The longer you’ve held these beliefs, the harder it will be to extract them out of your life and replace them with the belief systems you need to take you to the next level or direction you want to go


## Limiting Beliefs

3 main categories:

1. __Hopelessness__
    1. It won’t happen for me; no use trying; this is for other people, not me
    2. __outcome__: See opportunity, want to join, but don’t.  Make other excuses for why
2. __Helplessness__
    1. I don’t have the ability to do it; I’m not smart enough or talented enough
    2. __outcome__: Might even join, run into one obstacle and fears are confirmed. They quit.
3. __Worthlessness__
    1. I’m not good enough; this is not for someone like me; they won’t like me
    2. __outcome__: First, even slight, sting of rejection or disapproval they quit

The truth is:  the belief "I’m not", is a lie. Before we ever agreed to believe I’m Not, it was someone else’s opinion.

Belief is a __SKILL__.  Born with no beliefs - all learned.  You can reshape your beliefs.  Unlock prison doors.
Change your beliefs, change your life.  Perfect example is the 4 minute mile.  It was believed that it was impossible, but someone believed they could and reached it.

Changing your beliefs:  A 4 step process.

1. __Identify__ what beliefs you need to hold in order to reach your goal(s)
    1. What do you need to believe about yourself, about other people, about this opportunity, about the world around you, etc. in order to get to royal ambassador?
    2. What beliefs do you need to uproot?
2. __Decide__ to draw a line in the sand and say that is it. This is over, I will not believe that any longer (unproductive beliefs)
3. __Immerse__ - you have to bombard your conscious/subconscious mind with other beliefs, with the beliefs that you want reinforced
    1. Use information, read success magazine, listen to inspirational audios
    2. Associations, we become the combined average of the 5 people we hang around the most, so get around people that are up in life
4. __ACT__ - just do the thing that you are afraid of. Courage is not the absence of fear.  It’s feeling the fear and doing it anyway.  Take steps forward
