OSSU Blog 2 - Update from Feb 1, 2026 until May 1, 2026

Thu 07 May 2026 in OSSU Blog
#ossu

It’s been about 3 months since my last blog update. Did many things that I won't discuss all of here. Some programming related but no OSSU/CS. I did take writing and speaking courses from coursera though. Also, recently wrote a review of SPD.

What’s Next

Next up is to continue OSSU. The next round of courses I plan on taking are CS50x, Class-based Program Design, and Math for CS (I only completed unit 1 last time I worked on OSSU).

Edit: I came across the Software Construction course from MIT. After discovering that there is some overlap with Class-Based Program Design, I decided to also take this course for my next session, for a total of 4 courses concurrently. There is a more recent version that uses TypeScript, and also a software construction course from CMU. I plan to take those two concurrently after I finish the Core Programming section. It'll likely be those ones, MIT's Missing Semester, and maybe SICP, concurrently.

But before continuing OSSU, I want to invest some time into learning how to learn. There’s this book called Ultralearning: The Essential Guide To Mastering Hard Skills And Future-Proofing Your Career. I want to read it, take notes, blog about it, and apply it. I also have a bunch of bookmarks for this topic that I want to go through one by one, taking notes and blogging about it as well.

Immediately next: learning how to learn.

Then: continue OSSU.

Then: Take a break to reflect, write next OSSU blog, and think about what to do next.
